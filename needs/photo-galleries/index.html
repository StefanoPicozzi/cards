Photo Galleries

Self-hosted alternatives to Google Photos, Amazon Photos and Flickr.

What people say

  • back up my phone photos
  • a private place for family photos
  • keep sensitive photos off the cloud
  • search my photos by face or place

Why people self-host it

People leave Google Photos, iCloud Photos, Amazon Photos, Flickr for it. Why, strongest first: privacy, cost, control.

Does it suit a machine of your own?

Fits, with conditions good with enough disk and a backup plan; photos are irreplaceable, so the backup is not optional

Questions that decide it

  • how sensitive are they, and do you need them away from home?
  • how much space does your library take?

How to publish it

Usually
none, with the app's own login, because phone backup apps cannot pass an Edgible sign-in (the immich card does this)
When the data is private or sensitive
offer three: not published (back up on home Wi-Fi only), org for the web with phone uploads at home, or none with a strong login and prompt updates. Add disk encryption and an encrypted off-site backup.
When the data is regulated
photos of patients, clients, or children carry legal duties: a prototype with consented or test photos only; real data needs compliant hosting and advice

Who sees what inside it

albums and partner sharing decide which family members see which photos; check sharing defaults before inviting people

The machine

lots of disk (library plus about 20% for thumbnails); 6 GB memory for Immich; a GPU helps face search but is optional

For builders

a photo store with face and place search, for a product prototype that works with people's photos
